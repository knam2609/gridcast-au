## Disclosures

- The served intervals are not M3's pre-registered headline but its reported-only 'CQR, rearranged' row: the same bounds sorted per pair so the levels nest, a transform added after M3's step-3 results were seen. (`reports/m3_intervals.md`, `reports/m3_holdout.md`)
- The development forecasts are M2's second development run; two runs looked at the same period, so development numbers are slightly optimistic, unlike the holdout's. (`reports/m2_demand.md`, `reports/m2_holdout.md`)
- Anomaly recipe v2 was designed after v1's development verdicts, so its development verdicts are post hoc; the holdout year is its only blind test. (`reports/m4_anomalies_v2.md`, `reports/m4_holdout.md`)
- Price forecasts are M1's baselines (persistence and the 28-day median profile), not a model, and have no intervals. (`reports/m1_baseline.md`)
- This is an archive, not a live service: nothing issued after the holdout year is served (see `splits`). (`docs/plans/m5_plan.md`)
- **P6 is also judged against the container** (step 5a): §3 judges every criterion on the Compose service, so `api-verify` repeats the contract checks of the test suites there, whose tests stay.
